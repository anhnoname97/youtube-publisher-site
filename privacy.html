Privacy Policy

Video Publisher · Effective October 28, 2026

Video Publisher is a desktop application that runs on the user's own Windows computer. The developer operates no server for it. No data from the application is sent to the developer.

YouTube API Services

Video Publisher uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube through this application is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's collection and use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions requested

  • youtube.upload: upload videos to the channel you choose.
  • youtube.readonly: read your channel ID and name to confirm the right channel is connected, and read the status of videos the application uploaded (processing, scheduled, public, rejected).

The application does not read your comments, subscribers, analytics, email or any other Google data.

Data the application stores

All of the following stays on your computer:

  • The OAuth refresh token, encrypted with Windows Data Protection API so that only your Windows account on that computer can decrypt it. Access tokens are kept in memory only.
  • Your channel ID, channel name and uploads playlist ID.
  • For each video it uploads: the YouTube video ID, the title and description sent, and status information returned by the API, together with timestamps and error messages.
  • Information about the local video files (path, size, checksum, duration) taken from your own computer.

Status data received from the YouTube API is refreshed periodically and at least every 30 days while the channel stays connected.

Sharing

The application sends data only to Google, through the YouTube Data API and Google's OAuth endpoints, to perform the uploads and status checks you configure. It does not sell, share or transfer data to anyone else, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Disconnect the channel inside the application: the token is revoked with Google and deleted from your computer.
  • Or revoke access at any time on the Google Account permissions page.
  • The local record of uploaded videos is kept as your upload history. You can delete it by deleting the application's data folder.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
